Accent Kit

Twelve accents from across the UK, plus General American. Each kit gives you real recordings to listen to, then the setting, zone and tone, the consonants, the vowels and ways into the accent.

Pick the mp3s from your audio folder (you can select them all at once). They stay on this device and are never uploaded.

Recordings belong to their archives and play on their own sites: the International Dialects of English Archive (IDEA, dialectsarchive.com), Seeing Speech and Dynamic Dialects (University of Glasgow, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons. This app keeps IDEA recordings only on your own device, for personal study as IDEA's terms allow. They are never uploaded with the app.
